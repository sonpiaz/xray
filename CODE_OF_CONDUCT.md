# Code of Conduct

XRay is an open community building research tools for AI agents and humans. We want everyone — contributors, maintainers, and users — to feel welcome and respected here.

## What we expect

When you participate in XRay's community spaces (GitHub repository, issue threads, pull requests, future discussion channels), please:

- **Engage thoughtfully.** Read carefully before replying. Ask for clarification when something is unclear.
- **Welcome newcomers.** Many contributors are learning OSS workflows for the first time. Default to patience and explanation over dismissal.
- **Focus on the work.** Critique ideas, code, and designs — not the person behind them. Disagreement is healthy; personal attacks are not.
- **Credit others.** When building on someone's work, link to it. When learning from a comment, say so.
- **Respect maintainer time.** XRay is volunteer-led. Maintainers will close stale issues, decline out-of-scope features, and merge on their own schedule.

## What we ask you to avoid

Conduct that makes the community unsafe or unwelcoming is not OK here. Maintainers may edit, remove, or hide contributions — or restrict accounts — that don't align with this spirit. Repeated or severe issues may result in a permanent ban from the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness something that crosses a line, email **sonxpiaz@gmail.com** privately. Include:

- A short description of what happened
- Where it happened (issue URL, PR URL, etc.)
- Any relevant screenshots or logs

We aim to acknowledge within 72 hours and follow up with the parties involved. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Scope

This document applies to all XRay-related spaces: the GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, code comments), any chat or forum channels we may create, and one-on-one communication that arises from project work.

## Inspired by

These guidelines draw from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and broader OSS community norms. We chose a tighter, project-specific version because we wanted the document to read like the project itself: opinionated, focused, and short.

---

By participating in XRay, you agree to uphold these guidelines and help us build a community worth contributing to.
